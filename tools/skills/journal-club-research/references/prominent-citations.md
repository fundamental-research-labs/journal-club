# Prominent citations and thesis coverage

Use this check when reading sources and when forming, shortening, or materially
revising a topic-wide thesis. Preserve the predecessors, comparisons, and challenges
needed to understand the argument. Citation popularity is a discovery signal, not
a measure of truth or a requirement to cite everything.

## Keep a list for each reviewed source

Maintain `research/prominent-citations.md` or an equivalent linked record, grouped
by the citing source. Link each substantive reading note to its list. A structured
companion is useful when it makes deduplication and aggregation reliable; avoid a
second source register. Discovery-only candidates need no empty lists.

Inspect the introduction and related work **and** the method, experimental baseline,
and limitation sections. Capture references the source relies on to explain its
contribution, test its claims, or motivate a limitation. Select on role and context;
neither copying the bibliography nor counting textual mentions establishes prominence.
There is no per-paper citation quota. Include already-known sources.

For each selected edge record:
- Citing source key, exact version or dated snapshot, inspected sections and access
  limits. Distinguish inspected/no relevant citations from not inspected.
- Cited title, canonical URL/DOI, and existing source/family key when available.
  Resolve ambiguous names and bibliography identities before merging.
- Role: direct predecessor, tested baseline, competing method, foundation, or
  limitation/counterevidence; multiple roles are possible.
- Page/section/table or paragraph locator and a short paraphrase of why the citing
  authors use it. Label this as the citing authors' characterization.
- Separate access status for the cited work. Reading a citation context does not
  verify the cited paper's findings.

Before accepting worker edges, reopen the actual citation context and bibliography.
Topic similarity and a prior note's full-text status are not evidence that an edge
exists or that the current worker inspected it. Check the cited work's first release
against the citing version's date; a later revision can add citations, but never
silently attribute them to an earlier version. Reject unresolved or impossible edges
from the aggregate and record the gap. Keep current-pass inspection scope separate
from inherited empirical reading depth.

Use first-party linked antecedents for practitioner sources when available; a
source with no bibliography is not deficient. Preserve original-source retention
rules. On updates, reuse verified edges and record version changes rather than
silently treating newer references as present in the earlier version.

## Aggregate and decide

Group incoming edges by canonical evidence family, deduplicating versions and
companion artifacts. Count distinct citing families once each, not repeated
mentions. Show the citing seeds and their roles so a reader can inspect the result.
Describe overlapping authors, shared projects, surveys, and inherited citation
lists where relevant. Do not label distinct papers as independent groups without
checking; mark independence unknown when it has not been assessed.

Review recurring substantive citations across different research groups, plus every
anchor's direct predecessor, decisive baseline, and strongest challenge even when
it has only one incoming edge. These are review triggers, not automatic inclusion
rules or numerical cutoffs. Keep independent discovery lanes: a citation network
can miss uncited work, recent releases, and practitioner contributions.

Reconcile each consequential candidate against the **actual essay**, not merely the
register or shortlist. In the coverage audit record one of:
- Integrated: where it explains a mechanism, establishes a comparison, or changes
  the conclusion; a bare name or bibliography entry is insufficient.
- Supporting only: the specific overlap or scope reason, the source representing
  that role in the essay, and why omission does not distort novelty or evidence.
- Research needed: the missing original or unresolved question, affected claim,
  and current qualification. Do not count an unread lead as reviewed evidence.

“Additional comparator,” “outside the top ten,” and “already in the corpus” do not
justify exclusion. Read consequential additions before reporting their findings;
merge their records using existing source keys. Report unreviewed citation leads
separately from the substantively reviewed evidence base.

## Check after rewriting

Repeat the reconciliation after condensation and after adding a new anchor. Verify
that the reader can explain what each anchor inherits, what it changes, and what
its main comparison establishes. Report the actual seed coverage, unresolved
identities/access gaps, and bounded stopping condition. A partial edge sample is
not a field-wide citation ranking or evidence of search saturation.
