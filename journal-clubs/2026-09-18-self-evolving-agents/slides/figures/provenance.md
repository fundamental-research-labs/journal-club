## This revision's source inspection

- **FinEvo, slide 8:** Figure 1(a) shows task coverage. The visible guidance separately explains the §4.1 paired retained/reset protocol; the figure is not claimed to depict that protocol. The crop was expanded after render inspection to preserve the complete panel border. Figure 1(b), the file workspace, is omitted. Table 5 is on PDF page 6; older reading notes' page-7 locator is historical.
- **AgentStream, slide 14:** re-read v1 PDF page 11, Table 5/caption, Table 6 and §5.4. All five methods and all columns are retained. Table 5 averages each method over three models and three order seeds; its last two columns count scenario winners out of nine configurations. The source writes gain (%); differences are percentage points over the no-evolution baseline. No confidence interval is supplied in the table. Authors' causal explanation is narrowed to a method-by-stream association because methods differ in more than retrieval.
- **SEAL, slides 15–17:** inspected original Figure 1 and §3, Table 2, complete Figure 6, §5 pp.8–9 and Appendix B.6. Figure 6's original accuracy cells, axes and color scale are intact. Later edits often degrade prior passage answers; there is no claim that every adjacent cell decreases. Entrywise SEMs are in B.6, with the nearby repeat denominator unclear. The sequential-edit experiment is distinct from Table 2.
- **Hyperagents, slides 20–21 and 25:** re-read v1 Figure 1/caption and §3; original left/central loops are retained and right-hand implementation traces omitted. Existing Figures 3/4 retain all test comparators and intervals. Gain at 50 candidate iterations and score at 200 continued-evolution iterations remain different endpoints. The latter is in the appendix with explicit blue/orange reading guidance; the green alternative is preserved.

## Calculations and intentional synthesis

- Slide 9 computes recorded execution + reflection tokens from the original FinEvo Table 5: skill-only **17.53 + 44.03 = 61.56**, full evolution **16.31 + 60.19 = 76.50**, both in 10⁴ tokens/task. Fixed expert skill has **15.92** execution and no reflection cost. The skill-only/full scores are 93.71/89.47; fixed expertise is 86.67. These columns exclude judging and expert authoring, so they are not a lifetime cost estimate. No uncertainty or break-even volume is invented.
- Slide 26 is an explicitly calculated summary from AgentStream Tables 2 and 11–13. [The existing reconstruction](../../research/check-agentstream-aggregates.py) documents means and positive/negative/tie counts. A presentation-specific aggregation is the reason for the editable table. Forty-five cells share 300 tasks; they are not independent datasets, a causal estimate, or a confidence interval.
- Slide 2's financial-report example is explicitly illustrative, not a claimed source experiment. Slides 3–5, 12, 19 and 22–24 provide conceptual framing, conditional synthesis and proposed decisions/tests. They do not replace any paper's conceptual artwork.
- Slides 4, 23, 27–29 and 31 are cross-source mechanism/evaluation comparisons, not redrawn empirical tables. Slide 18 is an attributed production-architecture summary; separate evaluation is our recommendation. Slide 30 retains the scoped SkillsBench curated-versus-one-shot explanation, with no invented iterative-learning comparison.
- Former R-Zero/Continual Internalization and Dream-RSI standalone slides were removed to allow fuller explanation of the selected methods. Their evidence, source notes and qualifications remain in the research corpus. No figure was replaced by generated or synthetic evidence.

## Regeneration

```sh
python3 journal-clubs/2026-09-18-self-evolving-agents/slides/build.py
```

Optional extraction uses PyMuPDF through `uv`; it reads retained originals unchanged and fetches missing source files into a temporary directory. Full regeneration:

```sh
uv run --with pymupdf python journal-clubs/2026-09-18-self-evolving-agents/slides/figures/extract.py
```
