## Concept and result interpretation

- **FinEvo 8–10:** slide 8 uses the actual report error from Tables 25–26 and the saved instruction in Figure 10. Its caption labels the omitted instruction rows. This is an observed change, not evidence that this particular change prevented later mistakes. Slide 9 defines three comparison conditions in text; it does not recreate a source mechanism diagram or an empirical chart. Slide 10 retains complete Table 5 and its row definitions. The complete original Figure 1 overview and task workspace remain on appendix slide 37.
- **FinEvo calculations:** gross margin (115.05 − 67.88) / 115.05 rounds to 41.00%, not the reported 40.02%. Skill-only minus fixed expertise is 93.71 − 86.67 = 7.04 rubric points; the title rounds this to 7. Recorded execution-plus-reflection totals: skill-only (17.53 + 44.03) × 10⁴ = 615,600, fixed expertise 15.92 × 10⁴ = 159,200, full evolution (16.31 + 60.19) × 10⁴ = 765,000 tokens/task. Visible 616k, 159k and 3.9× are rounded; the ratio is 3.8668. Judging and expert authoring are excluded. The alternatives are distinct conditions, not sequential checkpoints or learning added to expert initialization.
- **WikiSkill 6–7, harness 11–12, AgentStream 14–15, SEAL 16–18, Hyperagents 21–22/26:** existing source versions, crops and numerical evidence are unchanged. Copy now explains actions and comparison conditions before interpretation. The method-overview figures remain original artwork. Hyperagents' gain after 50 iterations is distinct from its continued-evolution endpoint after 200.
- **AgentStream 27:** an intentional calculated cross-condition summary, not a redrawn single-source figure. [The reconstruction](../../research/check-agentstream-aggregates.py) checks Tables 2 and 11–13. Forty-five cells reuse 300 tasks; counts are not independent datasets or confidence intervals. Original method-level evidence is on slide 15.
- **Illustrations and synthesis:** slide 2 is explicitly illustrative. Slides 3–5, 13, 20 and 23–25 are conceptual framing, synthesis and proposed tests. Tables on slides 4, 24 and 28–30/32 compare concepts or protocols. Slide 19 attributes the company-described process; 31 scopes the one-shot skill-authoring comparison. None presents generated imagery as evidence.

## Regeneration

Normal HTML builds use Python 3's standard library and local assets only:

```sh
python3 journal-clubs/2026-09-18-self-evolving-agents/slides/build.py
```

Optional extraction uses PyMuPDF through `uv`; it reads retained originals unchanged and fetches missing source files into a temporary directory. Full regeneration:

```sh
uv run --with pymupdf python journal-clubs/2026-09-18-self-evolving-agents/slides/figures/extract.py
```

To regenerate only new/cropped assets while preserving other manifest entries:

```sh
uv run --with pymupdf python journal-clubs/2026-09-18-self-evolving-agents/slides/figures/extract.py --only finevo-figure-10-check finevo-figure-1a hyperagents-figure-1-loops agentstream-table-5 seal-figure-6
```
