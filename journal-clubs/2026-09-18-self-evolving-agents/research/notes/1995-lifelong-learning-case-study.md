## Mechanism, interpretation and limits

The framework transfers learned bias across related concept-learning tasks. Its support-set assumption keeps earlier datasets available. This is conceptual background, not a claim of retention under restricted memory. Object-recognition results are not quantitatively reviewed here. The connection to agent skills is analyst comparison, not documented direct descent.
