# EvoX: Meta-Evolution for Automated Discovery

## Source and access

Key: `2026-evox`. [Primary v2](https://arxiv.org/html/2602.23413v2); [register](../sources.md#2026-evox). First 2026-02-26; version 2026-03-16. Read September 16, 2026: methods, main results, relevant appendices. [Retained original](../originals/2026-evox/2026-evox-paper-v2.pdf); CC BY 4.0.

## Question and methods

Can the search strategy evolve alongside candidate solutions? A stagnation-triggered outer loop changes selection and variation while retaining the solution population; the evaluator and foundation models remain fixed (§§3–4).

## Results and evidence

§6 evaluates 196 tasks: 8 math, 6 systems, 10 ALE-Bench-Lite, 172 Frontier-CS. Open methods generally receive 100 iterations; math/system tables report mean and best over three runs. Frontier-CS mean/median are 62.6/75.5 (Figure 3). §6.4 compares evolving versus fixed strategies on one math task.

## Appraisal and limitations

Coordinator: useful meta-search comparator, but iteration parity is not total-compute parity. AlphaEvolve values come from prior work with unknown budget; Figure 3 lacks run dispersion. The one-task cost study cannot establish universal cost savings. Reserve alongside Dream-RSI, preserving Hyperagents as the selected meta-improvement anchor.

## Discussion and follow-up

What persists across tasks, as opposed to improving search within one task? Would strategy evolution still win with all proposal, strategy-generation, and evaluator costs matched?

Results above are author-reported; appraisal is coordinator interpretation. No experiments reproduced. [Screening detail](../workers/citation-screening-meta.md).
