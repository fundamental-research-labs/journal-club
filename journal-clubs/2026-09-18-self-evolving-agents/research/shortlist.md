# Priority resources: self-evolving agents

**Prepared September 16, 2026 for the September 18 session.** This is a ranked research selection for a topic-wide journal club, not ten consecutive paper reviews. Priority reflects complementary mechanisms, useful controls, teaching value, and evidence quality. Newness alone does not determine rank. All ten have substantive primary-source reading notes; author claims remain distinct from the interpretation below.

**Start with WikiSkill, the harness-evaluation critique, SEAL, and Hyperagents.** Together they establish persistent experience, a meaningful counterfactual, weight adaptation, and improvement of the improver. Add AgentStream and R-Zero to test whether progress lasts. Shopify supplies a production systems perspective with limited public causal evidence.

## Ranked ten

| Rank / resource | Why read it; evidence worth discussing | Main boundary / reading guide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **1. [WikiSkill](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.27454v1)** — Aug 27, 2026 preprint | A clear positive example of turning experience into persistent wiki knowledge and executable skills. Across five models and five benchmarks, Qwen-3.5-9B macro accuracy rises **29.9→47.4**; three evolution runs and held-out splits. | Some regressions: 4B OfficeQA **30.2→28.5**. Full skill injection bypasses retrieval, and small validation sets govern acceptance. Read §§3–4, Tables 1/3, Appendix B–C. [Detailed note](notes/wikiskill.md). |
| **2. [Rethinking the Evaluation of Harness Evolution for Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.12227v2)** — Jul 14; v2 Aug 27, 2026 preprint | The essential counterfactual: could the same rollout budget simply buy more attempts at the original task? Reported averages favor sampling over evolution (**72.3 vs 67.4**); a 45/10/34 train/validation/test split yields only **68.3 vs 67.7** on held-out tasks. | Two runs, a small held-out set, and one implementation. Five-rollout matching is not full token/dollar parity. This challenges a common evaluation, not every possible evolution method. Read main comparisons and held-out experiment. [Note](notes/harness-evolution-evaluation.md). |
| **3. [GEPA](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.19457v2)** — Jul 25, 2025; v2 Feb 14, 2026; ICLR 2026 Oral | Teaches reflective mutation, trace feedback, and Pareto selection in prompt space. Six-task Qwen aggregate reported in Table 1: **54.85 GEPA vs 48.91 GRPO**, with task-dependent **1,839–7,051 vs 24,000 rollouts**. | Rollouts do not equal total compute; no independent-run uncertainty in the main tables. GEPA loses to GRPO on AIME (**32 vs 38**). Pair with [VISTA's seed-failure challenge](notes/2026-reflection-in-the-dark.md). Read Algorithm 1, Tables 1–3, Appendix E. [Note](notes/2025-gepa.md). |
| **4. [Self-Adapting Language Models / SEAL](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.10943v2)** — Jun 12; v2 Sep 18, 2025; NeurIPS 2025 | The clearest contrast to external memory: a model generates data and update instructions that actually change its weights. On selected ARC tasks, **72.5% of sampled self-edit attempts succeed**; knowledge-incorporation experiments and forgetting tests expose a second use case. | ARC uses **11 training tasks, 8 test tasks, five edits/test task**: 40 attempts, not the full ARC benchmark. Knowledge incorporation uses two outer RL rounds; subsequent updates can degrade earlier-task performance. Read §§3–4 and Appendix A–B. [Note](notes/2025-self-adapting-language-models.md). |
| **5. [Hyperagents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.19461v1)** — Mar 19, 2026 preprint | Directly asks whether the code that improves an agent can itself improve. Task and meta agents share an editable program; five-run experiments and cross-domain tests make it a useful meta-improvement anchor. | Fixed outer selection and evaluators still anchor the loop. The reported mathematical transfer endpoint **0.640 vs 0.610 is not statistically significant**. Five-run bootstrap intervals and one-sided tests are audited in the note; no evidence of indefinite compounding. Read mechanism, §5, Figure 4, and statistical details. [Note](notes/hyperagents.md). |
| **6. [AgentStream](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.00155v1)** — Jul 31, 2026 preprint | Tests retained-state methods under isolated, sequential, and interleaved streams: six benchmarks × 50 tasks, three models, five methods, three order seeds. Mean differences from each model’s vanilla macro average shrink from **+1.37 to +0.75/+0.90 percentage points** across these scenarios. | The 45 model–method–seed cells per scenario share tasks and are not independent replications. Positive cells coexist with many regressions; lower action counts can conceal much higher memory cost. Read Tables 1–2, 7, 11–13. [Note](notes/agentstream.md); [aggregate audit](check-agentstream-aggregates.py). |
| **7. [R-Zero](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.05004v4)** — Aug 7, 2025; v4 Feb 13, 2026; ICLR 2026 | Adds autonomous curriculum generation and alternating Challenger/Solver weight updates. Its most useful result is the limit: Qwen3-4B two-model average math performance peaks at **49.12 at step 45**, then falls to **46.52 at step 60** (Appendix Table 6). | Retrospective model-judge audits find declining pseudo-label quality, but that association does not isolate the cause of collapse. Several main-table averages conflict with the prose; no training-run intervals or complete compute ledger. Pair with [Absolute Zero](notes/2025-absolute-zero.md) for executable self-play rewards. Read §§2/4 and Appendix D–E. [Note](notes/2025-r-zero.md). |
| **8. [HarnessDev](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.01437v1)** — Sep 1, 2026 preprint | Autonomous harness creation and evolution with a separate held-out test: 189 feedback tasks, 630 held-out SWE-Pro tasks, nine lineages. Useful for asking whether the system can select versions that actually generalize. | Feedback and held-out changes agree in direction on **34/64 adjacent version switches**—not 34 proven improvements. Five self-runtime lineages improve, while only one of four fixed-runtime lineages does; each lineage is a single trajectory. Read evolution setup and version-switch analysis. [Note](notes/harnessdev.md). |
| **9. [FinEvo-Bench](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.06144v1)** — Aug 6, 2026 preprint | A valuable paired persistence control outside coding: four scaffolds use the same backbone across three shuffled streams of **120 financial tasks / 20 recurring scenes**, compared with state reset before every task. Reported gains **9.33–19.37 points** on 100-point rubrics; compliance issues also fall. | Main effects lack across-run intervals. A single expert calibrates one run's judge scores; recurring authored procedures limit generalization. **775 refers to input files**, not independent outputs. Judge tokens are excluded from cost. Read §§3–4, Tables 3/5/6. [Note](notes/2026-finevo-bench.md). |
| **10. [Shopify: Sidekick's continual learning loop](https://shopify.engineering/sidekicks-continual-learning-loop)** — Aug 5, 2026 engineering account | An original production account connecting harness search, repaired trajectories, SFT, GRPO, daily weight updates, and prompt compression. It shows the operational work around a deployed learning loop. | Public quality ablations, denominators, split manifests, and raw traces are absent. Serving cost and throughput improvements cannot be attributed solely to learning. Use for architecture and deployment constraints, not a benchmark victory. [Note](notes/2026-shopify-sidekick.md). |

### Selection rubric

Ratings are qualitative judgments, not a summed score or a probability. **R** = relevance; **E** = empirical evidence for the broad topic; **N** = distinct mechanism/question; **T** = teaching value; **C** = contribution to this selection. H/M/L = high/medium/low; U = unassessed. Evidence can be medium despite peer review when repeat uncertainty, cost matching, or transfer is limited. A source with low public efficacy evidence can still be valuable for an architectural claim.

The high coverage ratings mean each fills a deliberate slot; they do not imply exhaustive coverage. No selected source independently demonstrates long-horizon, domain-general, accelerating recursive improvement. That is a scope assessment of these experiments, not a proof that such improvement is impossible.

## Strong alternatives and mandatory challenge companions

These are useful research resources, not an implied assignment to read everything. The first four are the closest substitutions if the session emphasis changes.

| Resource | When to use it / why it is outside the ten |
| --- | --- |
| **[Dream-RSI](notes/2026-dream-rsi.md)** — Sep 14, 2026 | First alternate for meta-improvement: discovery histories become replay environments for evolving an exploration controller. A within-model Lasso comparison uses 317 rather than 550 discovery calls, but total cost/repeat uncertainty and controller transfer remain limited. Keeps the newest work visible; overlaps Hyperagents' shortlist role. |
| **[EvoHarnessBench](notes/evoharnessbench.md)** — v2 Sep 10, 2026 | First evaluation alternate for changing tool/API catalogs: 17 streams, 802 unique tasks, three-run population SDs. Adds environmental change to AgentStream's task-order change. These are different benchmarks; neither is the Evo-Harness method. |
| **[Library Drift / Ratchet](notes/library-drift.md)** — v3 Jul 29, 2026 | Best lifecycle example: default last-ten-minus-first-ten gain **+0.328±0.018**, harsh retirement **−0.019±0.010** across three seeds. Narrow selected MBPP tasks and different call budgets; use with WikiSkill to discuss deletion and reuse. |
| **[NVIDIA NemoClaw memory](notes/2026-nemoclaw-memory.md)** — Sep 4, 2026 | Stronger inspectable practitioner evaluation than Shopify, but narrower scope: 186 synthetic questions, same base model, gains plus faithfulness/single-hop regressions. Missing evaluated self-model adapter and incomparable ingestion accounting remain material. Substitute if artifact inspectability matters more than production-loop breadth. |
| [ACE](notes/2025-agentic-context-engineering.md) | Detailed online playbook mechanism and efficiency accounting. Substitute for GEPA if the session centers on context maintenance; AgentStream already tests ACE and supplies a counterexample. |
| [Huxley–Gödel Machine](notes/2025-huxley-godel-machine.md), [Darwin Gödel Machine](notes/2025-darwin-godel-machine.md) | Search/selection ancestry and resource accounting for code evolution. HGM has equal-evaluation comparisons and held-out SWE-Lite transfer; still single evolutionary runs. Related method lineage, not independent replications of Hyperagents. |
| [Evo-Harness](notes/2026-evo-harness.md), [Agentic Harness Engineering](notes/2026-agentic-harness-engineering.md) | More concrete harness mechanisms and self-feedback/oracle-feedback comparisons; substantial overlap with GEPA/HarnessDev. Preserve regressions and optimization-set reuse. |
| [Agent-World](notes/2026-agent-world.md), [World Knowledge Exploration](notes/2026-spontaneous-world-knowledge.md) | Explicit environment/curriculum generation and proactive mapping. Agent-World is the stronger coverage reserve, with broad external benchmarks but unmatched resources and no reported dispersion. The world-knowledge method creates deployment-time Markdown context; “reward-free” applies to inference, not training. |
| [Absolute Zero](notes/2025-absolute-zero.md) | Positive executable self-play companion to R-Zero. These are distinct studies, grouped for reading economy—not two reports of one experiment and not two independent confirmations of identical conditions. |
| [VISTA / Reflection in the Dark](notes/2026-reflection-in-the-dark.md) | Required challenge if presenting GEPA: constructed seed defect, dramatic recovery from a hand-designed hypothesis prior, one optimization seed. A counterexample, not a population reliability estimate. |
| [Ground Truth First](notes/2026-ground-truth-first.md), [Self-Evolution Backfires](notes/2026-self-evolution-backfires.md) | Memory attribution, provenance, clustering, and adaptive benchmark contamination. GTF's six-user primary clustered test is p=.063; Backfires uses one trajectory. Important qualifications, not decisive universal negative results. |
| [RewardHackingAgents / RHB](notes/2026-reward-hacking-agents.md), [STOP](notes/2023-stop.md) | Evaluator isolation and reward tampering. STOP also establishes the historical distinction between improving a fixed-model scaffolding program and changing the model itself. |
| [Reef](notes/reef.md), [autoresearch](notes/autoresearch.md), [SoL-Pi](notes/sol-pi.md) | Inspectable implementation loops. Reef's fresh same-task 22/60 versus 21/60 result is not held-out transfer; an executable recipe alone does not establish effective self-improvement. |
| [Escher-Loop](notes/2026-escher-loop.md) | Matched 10M equivalent-token budgets and three-run experiments make a useful meta-optimization comparison, but all evaluation reuses three fixed geometry instances and results are mixed by instance. |
| [SIA](notes/2026-sia.md), [MetaRSI](notes/metarsi.md), [ScienceBuddy](notes/sciencebuddy.md) | Frontier combinations of harness and weight updates. SIA exposes test-set optimization; MetaRSI's actual accounting ledgers remain unavailable; ScienceBuddy's released recipe has material overlap and differs from the paper schedule. Keep their headline results provisional. |
| [The Economics of Recursive Self-Improvement](notes/2026-economics-rsi.md) — Sep 14, 2026 | A theory/scenario resource for narrow versus broad acceleration and bottlenecks. The critical R&D-capability elasticity is not causally estimated; do not turn the illustrative threshold into an empirical agent result. |
| [Reflexion](notes/2023-reflexion.md), [Voyager](notes/2023-voyager.md), [AlphaEvolve](notes/2025-alphaevolve.md) | Historical verbal feedback, reusable code skills, and externally verified program search. Use to define what later work changes, rather than spending three additional reading slots. |
