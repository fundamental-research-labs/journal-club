## Strongest five for follow-up

1. **AHE** — closest direct competitor to existing HarnessDev, with explicit observability and transfer claims; needs full-text audit of independence, cost, and task splits.
2. **Evo-Harness** — latest online harness-learning system and likely most consequential missing August 2026 result; abstract has a malformed benchmark name and no denominators, so it is provisional.
3. **Huxley-Gödel Machine** — principled response to the current-score/metaproductivity mismatch; pair with DGM/SICA and test whether CMP estimates add signal under fixed compute.
4. **DGM** — canonical historical harness/code evolution family and major reported coding gains; inspect contamination, evaluator reuse, and archive-selection effects before treating gains as general.
5. **AlphaEvolve** — strongest adjacent industrial evidence for evaluator-driven evolutionary coding, but transfer to self-improving agents is an inference and its proprietary-scale results are not directly comparable.

## Main limitations and next searches

The lane found many abstracts and code leads but only partial full-text evidence; quantitative fields should remain provisional until the coordinator reads AHE, Evo-Harness, HGM, and DGM originals. Most families use benchmark feedback as both search objective and acceptance gate, so evaluator overfitting, repeated task exposure, model upgrades, and added inference budget remain confounds. Independent replication and rollback/regression evidence are sparse. Two bounded expansion passes produced the recent AHE/Evo-Harness/Living-Harness/SkillHone families and then mostly duplicate harness-search leads; further work should prioritize canonical full texts, cost ledgers, held-out task streams, and longitudinal code-quality metrics rather than more name collection.
