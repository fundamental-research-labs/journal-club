## Missing candidates and one expansion

1. **EvoX** is the strongest missing candidate. Its primary abstract says fixed search knobs fail to adapt and that EvoX jointly evolves solutions and their search strategies across nearly 200 optimization tasks, with comparisons to AlphaEvolve, OpenEvolve, GEPA, and ShinkaEvolve. This is decision-relevant evidence for whether Dream-RSI's replay simulator is a distinct contribution or one point in a broader meta-evolution design space. Verify full experiments before using any performance claim.
2. **MLEvolve** is a second strong omission. Its primary abstract reports Progressive MCGS graph reference edges, retrospective memory, adaptive coding modes, and MLE-Bench evaluation under a 12-hour budget. It is the closest cited alternative for cross-branch history reuse and hierarchical control. Full text and exact controls remain unread.

The bounded search also surfaced **SeaEvo: Advancing Algorithm Discovery with Strategy Space Evolution** (https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.24372; submitted 2026-04-27). This was a related-search discovery, not a verified backward citation from EvoX: I did not establish that EvoX cites SeaEvo. Its primary abstract describes persistent natural-language strategy state, stratified experience retrieval, and landscape navigation, making it a plausible adjacent comparator. It is a discovery lead only: no full-text review was completed and it should not be promoted on abstract claims alone.

No downloaded copyrighted full texts were retained by this worker. No citation-only edge is treated as evidence of a cited work's findings. Recommended register additions are EvoX and MLEvolve; SkyDiscover remains an unresolved citation lead pending a canonical primary page.
