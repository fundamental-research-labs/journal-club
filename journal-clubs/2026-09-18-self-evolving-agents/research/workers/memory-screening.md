# Memory, skill, and context optimization: Tier 2 screening

**Run/cutoff:** September 16, 2026. **Scope:** all 25 families in `memory-discovery.md`, screened independently for relevance (R), evidence strength (E), novelty (N), teaching/discussion value (T), and coverage contribution (C). H/M/L/U mean high/medium/low/unknown. “Provisional” means the judgment rests on metadata/abstract/README rather than primary full-text inspection. This worker did not alter the shared register or existing notes.

## Cross-batch recommendation

The strongest memory-lane shortlist candidates are **Ground Truth First**, **ACE**, **GEPA**, **WikiSkill**, **AgentStream**, **Library Drift**, **SelfMem**, **MemRL**, and **SkillOS**, with **VISTA** and **When Self-Evolution Backfires** as challenge sources. **ReasoningBank** and **MemSkill** are close reserves: both are highly relevant, but their positive results overlap the selected mechanisms and depend heavily on self-judging or benchmark-specific memory construction. The older foundations **LongMemEval** and **A-MEM** should remain available for historical framing; this prevents the story from becoming a list of 2026 names.

The coherent teaching arc is: editable contexts and memories can improve frozen agents (GEPA/ACE/MemRL/SelfMem), but gains depend on stream order, retention policy, seed quality, admission gates, and evaluation horizon (AgentStream/Library Drift/VISTA/contamination/Ground Truth First). LongMemEval supplies the older benchmark foundation; WikiSkill supplies controlled positive evidence with an explicit regression.

## Full-text priority checks

- **GEPA, v2 (2026-02-14), ICLR 2026 Oral.** Six tasks; explicit train/validation/test splits; Qwen Table 1 aggregate 54.85 versus 48.91 GRPO, with AIME regression 32 versus 38. Task-dependent 1,839–7,051 versus 24,000 GRPO rollouts, but rollouts are not matched total compute. No repeated-run uncertainty in main tables. Detailed note: [2025-gepa.md](../notes/2025-gepa.md).
- **ACE, v3 (2026-03-29), ICLR 2026.** AppWorld and finance with official splits; online runs adapt on a fixed shuffled test stream. Table 4 reports 75.1% fewer rollouts and 86.9% lower latency than GEPA; Appendix reports 204.1M→39.3M input and 1.87M→0.31M output tokens for the compared run. No order-seed uncertainty. AgentStream independently shows an ACE interleaved regression. Detailed note: [2025-agentic-context-engineering.md](../notes/2025-agentic-context-engineering.md).
- **ReasoningBank, v2 (2026-03-16).** WebArena has 812 tasks across five domains; Mind2Web tests cross-task/site/domain; SWE-Bench Verified is also used. Table 1 reports WebArena overall success 53.9 versus 44.1 AWM for Gemini-2.5 and fewer steps; MaTTS k=5 spends five trajectories/task. The framework self-judges trajectories, model/domain settings vary, and main tables lack repeated-run uncertainty. Reserve behind ACE/MemRL unless reasoning-memory distillation becomes central.
- **MemRL, v2 (2026-02-12).** Ten repeated epochs across five task settings; Table 1 average last/CSR .772/.798 versus .736/.760 MemP; Table 2 held-out transfer .794 versus .766. No main-table uncertainty; different backbones by benchmark; ambiguous multi-memory credit assignment. Detailed note: [2026-memrl.md](../notes/2026-memrl.md).
- **SelfMem, v1 (2026-07-04).** BEAM: 100 conversations, 2,000 probes at 100K/500K/1M. Table 1 scores .504/.487/.454 versus RAG .339/.346/.320, with explicit API-cost/request columns; no uncertainty. Strategy refinement uses conversations 0–8/9–19 only at 100K and peaks before its last iteration. Detailed note: [2026-selfmem.md](../notes/2026-selfmem.md).
- **MemSkill, v2 (2026-05-24).** LoCoMo is split 6/2/2; LongMemEval-S removes abstention and tests a stratified sample of about 100 after validation tuning; ALFWorld uses official seen/unseen splits. Table 1 shows broad gains, including LLaMA LoCoMo judge score 53.82 and ALFWorld average 80.36. Transfer to Qwen is useful, but LLM executors/designers/judges compound dependence; limited deployment/security evaluation. Reserve.
- **SkillOS, v1 (2026-05-07).** ALFWorld (3,553 train, 140 valid-seen test), WebShop, AIME24/25, GPQA; mean±1 SD over three runs. Training uses 16 H100s for roughly 2.5–5 days. Table 2 shows Qwen3-8B curator with Gemini executor average reasoning 88.6 versus 81.8 no-memory; Table 3 drops ALFWorld 61.2→57.3 without grouped streams. Group construction deliberately links related tasks and task outcomes use an LLM judge, so delayed-transfer claims need careful scope. Shortlist candidate.
- **Ground Truth First, v1 (2026-07-24).** Short horizon: 275 questions × 3 replicates = 825/backend. Tenure: 6 users × 3 replicates, n=324/cell at weeks 3/6/9. Map-vs-graph interaction is +17.3 pp, user-cluster CI +8.3 to +26.2, sign-flip p=.063; cross-family rejudge +24.1, all six users, p=.031. Full history ties/beats memory short-term and yields no judge-independent week-nine winner; layered memory uses about half the read tokens. The paper releases MIT generator/harness and CC BY 4.0 synthetic corpus in principle, but the artifact package was still “in preparation”; single-author, synthetic, no independent replication. High-value challenge/evaluation source.

## Metadata, access, copies, and uncertainty

All eight priority PDFs downloaded successfully to a temporary directory and were converted to text for inspection; no PDF was retained because this worker was constrained to the report/notes and arXiv paper redistribution licenses were not verified. GEPA, ACE, ReasoningBank, MemRL, and MemSkill version dates above supersede the lane's v1-only metadata. SkillOS, SelfMem, and Ground Truth First remain v1 as of the cutoff.

License evidence is artifact-specific. Ground Truth First states MIT for the generator/harness/Veracium and CC BY 4.0 for its synthetic corpus, while also saying the study-artifact package is still in preparation. The discovery lane says the MemSkill repository states Apache-2.0, but that repository claim was not re-audited here. For every other paper/code/data artifact, retention permission remains unknown until its license is inspected; public download alone is not a retention basis.

No candidate with metadata-only access should support a quantitative slide. The most consequential next reads are VISTA, When Self-Evolution Backfires, MemoryAgentBench's implementation, and Mem2ActBench. Those would test seed sensitivity, irreversible contamination, whether “selective forgetting” is operationalized faithfully, and whether memory improves action rather than QA.

## Targeted counterevidence verification addendum

Full reads changed both challenge sources from metadata-only to substantive access.

- **VISTA / Reflection in the Dark, v2 (June 8, 2026): promote to reserve challenge, and pair with GEPA if GEPA is selected.** Its GSM8K counterexample uses GEPA's official defective seed, 50/50 working train/validation examples, the sealed 1,319-example official test set, budget T=500, and the same seed conditions for both optimizers. The 23.81→13.50 GEPA degradation is credible for this configuration. It is not a broad reliability estimate: one random seed, two math benchmarks, and a handcrafted heuristic set that contains the relevant failure category. No independent optimization repeats or confidence intervals are reported. It complements GEPA directly but does not replace broader AgentStream or repeated Library Drift evidence. See [2026-reflection-in-the-dark.md](../notes/2026-reflection-in-the-dark.md).
- **When Self-Evolution Backfires, v1 (August 6, 2026): retain as reserve challenge.** The ungated 62→50 collapse and VaG 52→72 rise occur on Event-50, which also drives skill distillation across five correlated rounds. Holdout-14 tunes gates; untouched Test-25 demonstrates positive frozen-pool transfer but does not reproduce the phase transition. There is one evolution trajectory, k=3 task rollouts, and overlapping ~30-point Wilson bands. Its admission/descendant mechanism is useful, but Library Drift remains better repeated lifecycle evidence and AgentStream better broad conditional evidence. See [2026-self-evolution-backfires.md](../notes/2026-self-evolution-backfires.md).
- **Ground Truth First:** substantive note added at [2026-ground-truth-first.md](../notes/2026-ground-truth-first.md). Main-shortlist recommendation is unchanged, with an explicit “emerging/unreplicated” label and artifact-package-pending caveat.
