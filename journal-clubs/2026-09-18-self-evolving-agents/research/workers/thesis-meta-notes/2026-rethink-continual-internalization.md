# Rethinking Continual Experience Internalization for Self-Evolving LLM Agents

**Provisional worker record, superseded for thesis use by the shared notes/register and [Tier 2 corrections](../thesis-additions-screening.md).**


Chen, Yang, Fan, Nie, Sun, Zheng, Hu, Pan, Zeng, and Lin, arXiv:2606.04703v1, first submitted 2026-06-03, https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.04703. The arXiv PDF is 10 pages, v1 only (submission history: 3 Jun 2026); no redistribution copy retained because the arXiv license was not verified. Full primary PDF text was read for §§1–6, Figures 1–6, Tables 1–3, and Limitations.

The paper defines continual experience internalization as an iterative loop: policy πθ(k) produces trajectories, an experience pool E(k) is extracted, the experience-conditioned policy supervises a new experience-free student (Eq. 3, §3). It tests three dimensions: instance versus principle level experience; global versus step-wise injection; and on-policy versus off-policy context distillation (§3). Experiments use Qwen3-4B-Instruct-2507 and Qwen3-8B, a 15K corpus from five public web-reasoning datasets, and WebWalkerQA, GAIA-Text103, and BrowseComp-ZH (§4). WebWalkerQA overlaps the training corpus and is explicitly in-domain; GAIA and BrowseComp-ZH are out-of-domain (§4). Training uses lr 1e-5, batch 128, five epochs, 8× NVIDIA A800; inference temperature 0.7, max 100 interaction steps, 32,768-token context (§4).

Under iterative on-policy distillation, Figure 1 shows performance collapse across three iterations on all three benchmarks. Instance-level experience gives transient first-iteration gains that can fall below base; 74.4% of sampled items contain URLs/domains, 57.3% concrete numbers, and 93.9% query/entity strings, versus 84.0% principle items containing reusable strategy statements and 3.7% of instance items (§5.1). With principle experience, step-wise injection beats global injection in one iteration: in a Qwen self-generated setting, WebWalkerQA 23.2→31.2%, GAIA 16.8→22.7%, BrowseComp-ZH 4.5→5.2% (Table 1, §5.2.1). Across later iterations, global injection degrades; a third-iteration premature-answer rate is 63.82% global versus 0% step-wise (Table 2, §5.2.2–5.2.3).

The key positive result is the combined recipe—principle-level, step-wise, off-policy distillation—which Figure 6 reports as sustaining gains and preserving in-context experience use across three iterations. Off-policy uses teacher-generated trajectories plus rejection sampling; on-policy uses student-induced states and reverse KL (§3, §5.3). The updated on-policy student averages 21.9 assistant turns versus 2.5 base and 4.5 teacher turns (Table 3), indicating a practical rollout-cost penalty. The paper reports plots rather than uncertainty intervals or repeated-seed statistics; exact Figure 5/6 percentages are not tabulated in text.

This is a strong omission and direct challenge to unrestricted self-improvement: the default continual internalization procedure collapses, while a carefully engineered recipe restores bounded multi-iteration transfer. It supports durable future-task value within web reasoning, but does not establish domain-general acceleration. Limitations (§6) explicitly restrict evidence to web reasoning and leave experience-pool size, selector quality, and filtering criteria open. In-domain overlap, generated experience, fixed evaluators, two backbone sizes, three iterations, and absent uncertainty limit causal and generalization claims.
