# Industry References for Multi-Agent LLM Systems

This folder collects non-paper industry sources that are useful for the
multi-agent LLM literature review. It focuses on sources that would normally be
missed by a paper-only bibliography: engineering blogs, product launch posts,
platform documentation, and production case studies.

The sources are not treated as peer-reviewed evidence. Use them for:

- documenting how industry systems are actually being built and marketed;
- supporting historical claims about platforms, protocols, and deployment
  patterns;
- motivating design dimensions such as orchestration, observability,
  sandboxes, handoffs, memory, and human review;
- contrasting product claims with the manuscript's stricter evidence standard.

Files:

- `industry-source-map.md` - curated source table with manuscript placement notes.
- `suggested-manuscript-integration.md` - where the strongest blog posts could
  be cited in the current manuscript.
- `references.bib` - BibTeX-style entries for selected industry sources.

Recommended citation rule: cite these as industry evidence or product
documentation, not as empirical proof unless the post reports a controlled
evaluation with enough methodological detail to assess it.
