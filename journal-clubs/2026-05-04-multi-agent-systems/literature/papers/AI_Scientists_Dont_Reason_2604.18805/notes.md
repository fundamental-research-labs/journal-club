# Notes

## Why It Matters
This paper is useful when arguing that scientific-agent evaluation should inspect reasoning process, not just final task success. It gives a benchmark-backed critique of scaffold-only improvements: if the base model does not test, update, and integrate evidence, orchestration may not repair the failure.

## When To Cite
Cite it for claims about LLM scientific agents, scaffold-vs-model effects, epistemic reasoning failures, evidence non-uptake, hypothesis testing, and reliability under repeated trials.

## Key Terms
Corral; scientific agents; epistemic operations; evidence non-uptake; untested claims; belief update; convergent multi-test evidence; trace interventions; pass-hat; ReAct; structured tool calling.
