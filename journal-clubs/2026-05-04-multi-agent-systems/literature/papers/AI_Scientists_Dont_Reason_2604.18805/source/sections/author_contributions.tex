\noindent M.R.G., N.A., and K.M.J.\ jointly designed the Corral framework and the analysis pipeline; M.R.G. and N.A.\ led the implementation. Environments were developed as follows: spectroscopic structure elucidation and retrosynthetic planning by M.R.G.; catalyst surface construction, machine learning, and circuit inference by N.A.; atomic force microscopy by I.M. and S.M.; molecular dynamics by S.M. and C.G.; and inorganic qualitative analysis by A.A.\@ The reasoning-trace analysis was led by M.R.G.; the intervention analysis by N.A.; and the performance-based analysis, enabled by the Corral core, was led jointly by C.G., N.A., and M.R.G.\@ 
N.M.A.K.\ and K.M.J.\ acquired funding, administered the project, provided resources, and supervised the work. K.M.J.\ drafted the manuscript with substantial contributions from M.R.G.\ and N.A.; C.G.\ drafted the related-work section. All authors reviewed, edited, and approved the final version of the manuscript.
