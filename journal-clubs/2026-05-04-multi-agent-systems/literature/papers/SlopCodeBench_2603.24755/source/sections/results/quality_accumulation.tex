Our first question is whether agent trajectories accumulate quality issues under iterative self-extension. They do. \autoref{fig:accumulation-results} shows results across all evaluated settings. Erosion increases over problem progress in \erosionPctIncreasing\% of trajectories and verbosity in \vfTrajIncPct{}\%. The driver is not just more code; it is concentration of decision-point load into a growing set of high-complexity functions. Mean high-CC function count rises from \erosionHighCcCountFirstMean{} to \erosionHighCcCountLastMean{}, and mean maximum CC rises from \newErosionFirstCcMaxMean{} to \newErosionLastCcMaxMean{}.

\paragraph{Compounding in a single function.}
On \texttt{circuit\_eval}, Opus~4.6's \texttt{main()} grows \erosionCeMainCcMultiplier{}$\times$ in cyclomatic complexity over \erosionCeNumCkpts{} checkpoints, from \erosionCeMainCcFirst{} to \erosionCeMainCcLast{}, expanding from \erosionCeMainLinesFirst{} to \erosionCeMainLinesLast{} lines. \paragraph{Verbosity.} Structural duplication accounts for most of the growth, increasing by \vfCloneGrowthPct{}\% across \vfCloneIncPct{}\% of trajectories. AST-grep violation density grows a more modest \vfAstOverallGrowth{}\%.

\paragraph{Early design decisions compound.}
On \codesearch{}, all \abNumConfigs{} configurations score 100\% at $\ckpt{1}$ and $\ckpt{2}$, yet their implementations already diverge: some build extensible dispatch, others hardcode the initial rule kinds. At $\ckpt{3}$, AST-based metavariable patterns split the field into three tiers. \abCsTierOneModel{} retains \abCsTierOneRate\%, passing \abCsTierOnePassedTests{} of \abCsNumCpThreeTests{} tests. Four configurations cluster at \abCsTierTwoMin--\abCsTierTwoMax\%. GPT~5.2~Codex and GPT~5.3~Spark collapse to \abCsTierThreeRate\%, failing all \abCsTierThreeCoreFailed{} core tests. By $\ckpt{5}$, that tier's failures grow from \abCsTierThreeCpThreeFailures{} to \abCsTierThreeCpFiveFailures{}. Investment shows the same pattern. On \texttt{eve\_industry}, Opus~4.6 spends more than double any other configuration at $\ckpt{2}$ for exact fractional arithmetic, then posts three consecutive 100\% checkpoints and leads at $\ckpt{6}$ with \abEveBestCpSixRate\% versus \abEveSecondCpSixRate\%. On \texttt{dag\_execution}, a modular package at $\ckpt{1}$ accommodates four subsequent checkpoints without rework, finishing \abDagCpOneGap{} points above the runner-up.
