# Notes

## Why It Matters
Curie is a useful reference point for agentic science because it treats experimentation as a rigor problem, not only an autonomy problem. The paper gives a concrete architecture for keeping AI agents aligned with experimental plans, validating setup and execution steps, and preserving interpretable experiment state. It also contributes a benchmark where agents must execute and justify real research-style experiments across multiple computer-science domains.

## When To Cite
Cite when discussing AI agents for science, automated experimentation, experimental rigor, multi-agent research workflows, benchmark design for scientific agents, or systems that enforce validation and provenance around LLM-driven experiments. It is especially relevant when contrasting ad hoc prompt-based research agents with architectures that enforce methodical control, reproducibility checks, and structured experiment memory.
