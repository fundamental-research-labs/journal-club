\section{Reproducibility Statement}
The authors have made an extensive effort to ensure the reproducibility of the results presented in this paper. \textit{First}, the experimental settings, including training configurations, evaluation protocols, and model setup, are clearly described and detailed in Section \ref{sec:experimental_details}. \textit{Second}, the prompts for the mathematical reasoning task are detailed in Appendix \ref{sec:appendix_prompts} for clarity and reproducibility. \textit{Third}, the M500 dataset, all agent prompts on all tasks, other configurations, and the complete codebase are included in the supplementary materials to facilitate full reproducibility and future research.
