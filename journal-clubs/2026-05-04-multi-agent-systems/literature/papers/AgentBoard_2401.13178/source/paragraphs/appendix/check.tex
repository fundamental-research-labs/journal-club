\section{Annotation of sub-goals}
We develop multiple methods for annotating sub-goals, broadly divided into: (1) observation-based, and (2) attribute-matching methods (Webshop and Webarena). For observation-based tasks, we annotate $N$ necessary observations to accomplish a goal, where the obtained reward score is the ratio of completed sub-goals to $N$. For tasks in the web category, we directly use of the matching score.
