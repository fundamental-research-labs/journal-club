# Notes

## Why It Matters
This paper is a useful baseline for multi-agent LLM claims: a non-interacting ensemble of repeated LLM calls plus voting can deliver broad benchmark gains without roles, communication, or complex coordination. Its strongest contribution is not that voting is new, but that it systematically studies how far the simple scaling trick goes across reasoning and code-generation tasks and ties the gains to difficulty, model strength, and inference budget.

## When To Cite
Cite it for Agent Forest, sampling-and-voting inference, self-consistency beyond CoT, inference-time scaling with multiple LLM calls, simple baselines for multi-agent systems, smaller-model ensembles versus larger single models, and cost/accuracy tradeoffs in LLM evaluation.
