# Notes

## Why It Matters
This survey is a useful bridge between language-evolution work, deep multi-agent reinforcement learning, and modern agent communication. Its main value is cautionary: adding a communication channel or observing task success does not mean agents learned portable, human-like language. Emergent protocols must be tested for whether they are used, whether they generalize, and whether they remain interpretable outside the narrow training partnership.

## When To Cite
Cite it for overviews of deep emergent communication, referential games, continuous versus discrete communication channels, compositionality analysis, causal diagnostics for communication, language drift, and the gap between machine-machine co-adaptation and human-machine communication.
