# Notes

## Why It Matters
AgentVerse is an early general-purpose LLM multi-agent framework that tries to make collaboration more than fixed role-play. Its main contribution is the reusable loop of recruiting specialists, choosing a communication structure, executing in an environment, and feeding evaluation back into the next round. It is also useful because the paper pairs performance claims with observations about emergent coordination and safety-relevant failure modes.

## When To Cite
Cite it when discussing LLM multi-agent frameworks, dynamic role assignment, horizontal versus vertical agent communication, multi-agent tool use, coding-agent collaboration before SWE-specific branch-and-merge systems, or qualitative evidence of emergent behaviors in embodied multi-agent environments.
