\begin{abstract}
Despite enthusiasm for Multi-Agent LLM Systems (MAS), their performance gains on popular benchmarks are often minimal. This gap highlights a critical need for a principled understanding of why MAS fail. Addressing this question requires systematic identification and analysis of failure patterns. 
We introduce \dataset{}, a comprehensive dataset of 1600+ annotated traces collected across \numMASDomain popular MAS frameworks. \dataset{} is the first multi-agent system dataset  to outline the failure dynamics in MAS for guiding the development of better future systems. To enable systematic classification of failures for \dataset{}, we build the first \textbf{M}ulti-\textbf{A}gent \textbf{S}ystem Failure \textbf{T}axonomy (\taxonomy{}). We develop \taxonomy{} through rigorous analysis of 150 traces, guided closely by expert human annotators and validated by high inter-annotator agreement ($\kappa = \cohenskappainterannotatoraverage{}$). This process identifies \numFailureMode{} unique modes, clustered into \numFailureArea{} categories: (i) \fci{}, (ii) \fcii{}, and (iii) \fciii{}.
To enable scalable annotation, we develop an LLM-as-a-Judge pipeline with high agreement with human annotations. We leverage \taxonomy{} and \dataset{} to analyze failure patterns across models (GPT4, Claude 3, Qwen2.5, CodeLlama) and tasks (coding, math, general agent), demonstrating opportunities for improvement through better MAS design. Our analysis provides insights revealing that identified failures require more sophisticated solutions, highlighting a clear roadmap for future research. We publicly release our comprehensive dataset (\dataset{}), the \taxonomy{}, and our LLM annotator to facilitate widespread research and development in MAS.\footnote{\url{https://github.com/multi-agent-systems-failure-taxonomy/MAST}} \footnote{\url{https://huggingface.co/datasets/mcemri/MAST-Data}}
\end{abstract}
