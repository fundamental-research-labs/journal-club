# Notes

## Why It Matters
This is one of the more ambitious examples of an LLM agent system aimed at scientific discovery rather than task completion or paper writing. Its value for the corpus is the combination of a concrete multi-agent architecture, explicit test-time compute scaling, human expert oversight, and preliminary wet-lab follow-up across multiple biomedical domains.

## When To Cite
Cite it when discussing AI co-scientists, LLM-assisted hypothesis generation, scientist-in-the-loop research agents, tournament/debate-based self-improvement, biomedical agent validation, drug repurposing with LLMs, or the need to pair agentic scientific ideation with expert and experimental validation.
