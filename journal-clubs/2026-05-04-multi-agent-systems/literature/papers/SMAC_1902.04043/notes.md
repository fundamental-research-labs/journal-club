# Notes

## Why It Matters
SMAC became a central benchmark for cooperative MARL because it packages partial observability, decentralized execution, heterogeneous units, and coordination-heavy combat into a reusable suite. It is especially important for papers that need to show whether centralized-training/decentralized-execution methods scale beyond small matrix games or toy gridworlds.

## When To Cite
Cite it when using or discussing the SMAC benchmark, PyMARL, StarCraft II micromanagement tasks, cooperative Dec-POMDP evaluation, centralized training with decentralized execution, or baseline comparisons involving QMIX, COMA, VDN, IQL, and QTRAN.

## Key Terms
SMAC; StarCraft Multi-Agent Challenge; PyMARL; cooperative MARL; Dec-POMDP; centralized training with decentralized execution; partial observability; local observations; global state; micromanagement; focus fire; overkill; kiting; win rate; StarCraft II Learning Environment; SC2LE.
