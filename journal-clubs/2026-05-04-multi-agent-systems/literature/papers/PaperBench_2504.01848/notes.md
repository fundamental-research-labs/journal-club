# Notes

## Why It Matters
PaperBench is a central benchmark for autonomous ML R&D because it turns "replicate a research paper" into a structured, executable evaluation. It is harder and more realistic than benchmarks that provide an existing codebase or a narrow scoring function, while still giving evaluators a rubric-based path to partial credit and scalable grading.

## When To Cite
Cite it when discussing AI agents that conduct ML research, long-horizon paper replication, benchmarks for autonomous R&D capability, author-reviewed rubric evaluation, LLM-based judges for complex submissions, or the limits of current frontier agents on full research workflows. It is also the natural citation when using PaperBench or PaperBench Code-Dev as an evaluation target.
