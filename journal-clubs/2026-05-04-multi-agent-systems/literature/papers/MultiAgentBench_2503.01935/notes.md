# Notes

## Why It Matters
MultiAgentBench is useful because it treats multi-agent LLM evaluation as more than "did the final answer pass." It gives a benchmark-level vocabulary for task progress, communication, planning, role assignment, graph topology, and competitive dynamics across both practical tasks and social simulations. The paper is also a cautionary benchmark: coordination can help, but the reported ablations show that larger groups, longer runs, or more discussion can also create overhead.

## When To Cite
Cite this paper when discussing benchmarks for LLM-based multi-agent systems, evaluation of collaboration and competition, coordination protocols such as star/tree/graph/chain, milestone-based task tracking, MARBLE, or evidence that coordination scores and task scores should be measured separately. It is especially relevant when comparing multi-agent systems across heterogeneous domains rather than only coding or only social simulation.
