# Claims

## Claim 1
**Claim:** Agent Hospital operationalizes a closed-loop hospital simulacrum using LLM-powered patient, nurse, and doctor agents.

**Evidence:** The paper describes a virtual hospital with patient, nurse, and doctor agents, 16 functional areas, 42 doctor agents and four nurse agents, and an event sequence covering disease onset through triage, registration, consultation, examination, diagnosis, pharmacy, convalescence, and follow-up.

**Caveats/Scope:** The hospital is simplified: medical professional agents do not get sick, patients and medical records are synthetic, and the paper does not establish clinical deployment readiness.

**Source pointers:** Introduction; Simulacrum Construction; Figure 1; Figure 2; Appendix A.1

## Claim 2
**Claim:** MedAgent-Zero lets doctor agents improve without manually labeled target-benchmark data by combining synthetic patient generation, successful-case memory, and validated reflection from failures.

**Evidence:** Patient agents are generated by coupling LLMs with medical knowledge bases. Successful treatment cases are stored in a medical case base, while wrong answers trigger reflection, validation, and refinement before rules are added to an experience base. Both stores are retrieved during later decisions.

**Caveats/Scope:** The base LLMs are frozen, the synthetic data quality depends on the underlying knowledge bases and LLM generation, and validation still occurs inside the constructed framework rather than real clinical practice.

**Source pointers:** Agent Evolution; Patient Agent Generation; Doctor Agent Evolution; Figure 3; Figure 4; Appendix B.1-B.3

## Claim 3
**Claim:** Doctor-agent evolution improves virtual medical decision accuracy across clinical departments.

**Evidence:** In Table 3, after training on 20,000 virtual patients per clinical department and testing on a separate set of 200 synthetic patients per department, overall examination accuracy increases from 66.14% to 98.76%, and overall diagnosis accuracy increases from 76.98% to 95.31%.

**Caveats/Scope:** The test patients are synthetic and generated within the same broad simulacrum design, so these gains should be read as virtual-world performance rather than direct evidence of real patient care quality.

**Source pointers:** Scaling Laws in Evolution; Appendix C.4; Table 3

## Claim 4
**Claim:** Treating more virtual patients generally improves doctor-agent performance, but the trend can be noisy and diminishing.

**Evidence:** Figure 5b shows respiratory-department accuracy improving as the doctor agent treats up to 50,000 patient agents. Figure 9 shows cardiology and nephrology examination/diagnosis accuracy generally rising over 20,000 patients, with rapid early gains and a reported cardiology drop between 12,000 and 14,000 cases.

**Caveats/Scope:** The paper presents empirical curves for selected departments, not a formal scaling law across all settings. The authors also note a possible OpenAI API update affecting one trend change.

**Source pointers:** Scaling Laws in Evolution; Alignment between Virtual and Real Worlds; Appendix C.2-C.3; Figure 5b; Figure 8; Figure 9

## Claim 5
**Claim:** MedAgent-Zero transfers some virtual-world learning signal to MedQA in the reported experiments.

**Evidence:** Table 4 reports MedAgent-Zero outperforming Direct, CoT, MedAgents, and Medprompt across GPT-3.5, GPT-4, GPT-4o, and o1-preview settings where available. For GPT-4o, MedAgent-Zero reaches 92.22%, compared with 91.52% for Medprompt, 90.42% for CoT, 89.24% for MedAgents, and 88.22% for Direct.

**Caveats/Scope:** MedQA is a multiple-choice exam benchmark, not clinical practice. The non-hybrid MedAgent-Zero setting avoids MedQA training labels, while the hybrid variant explicitly adds real-world Q&A pairs.

**Source pointers:** Scaling Laws in Evolution; Alignment between Virtual and Real Worlds; Appendix C.5; Figure 5c; Table 4

## Claim 6
**Claim:** Both the medical case base and the experience base are useful components of MedAgent-Zero.

**Evidence:** The ablation in Figure 12 shows lower MedQA accuracy when either the experience base or the medical case base is removed, while the full MedAgent-Zero and hybrid variants perform better.

**Caveats/Scope:** The ablation is reported for MedQA and selected base models, so it does not prove the same component contribution for all medical tasks or deployments.

**Source pointers:** Appendix C.7; Figure 12
