# Notes

## Why It Matters
ACC-Collab is useful because it frames LLM collaboration as something to optimize directly. Instead of relying on generic agents to discover good debate behavior from prompts, it trains a small actor-critic team using trajectory-level signals about which messages make later correctness more likely.

## When To Cite
Cite it for trained multi-agent deliberation, actor-critic LLM teams, guided trajectory generation, DPO for collaborative agents, and empirical comparisons against Society of Minds, Persona, DebateTune, SFT, and DebateGPT on QA benchmarks.

## Key Terms
ACC-Collab; actor-agent; critic-agent; multi-agent deliberation; guided collaborative trajectories; partial trajectory reward; off-policy trajectory generation; Direct Preference Optimization; process accuracy; BoolQ; MMLU; BBH; SCIQ; ARC.
