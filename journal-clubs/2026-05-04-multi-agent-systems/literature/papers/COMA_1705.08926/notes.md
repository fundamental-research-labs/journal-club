# Notes

## Why It Matters
COMA is a canonical early deep MARL method for centralized training with decentralized execution. Its lasting contribution is the counterfactual advantage: a practical way to turn a global team reward into an agent-specific policy-gradient signal using a centralized critic. It is also a key reference point for StarCraft micromanagement as a cooperative MARL testbed.

## When To Cite
Cite COMA when discussing multi-agent credit assignment, centralized critics for decentralized policies, counterfactual baselines, cooperative policy gradients, or early StarCraft micromanagement benchmarks. It is especially useful when contrasting CTDE actor-critic methods with independent learners, value-factorization methods, or communication-based MARL.
