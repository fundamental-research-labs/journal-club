# Notes

## Why It Matters
This is a compact early example of language emerging as a coordination tool in multi-agent reinforcement learning. It is useful because the evaluation is grounded in task success, not linguistic plausibility, and because the paper studies how private goals, shared reward, embodiment, discrete symbols, and vocabulary pressure interact to produce compositional protocols.

## When To Cite
Cite it for emergent communication, grounded language learning without text supervision, compositional protocols in multi-agent RL, Gumbel-Softmax training of discrete communication, vocabulary-size bottlenecks, and non-verbal alternatives to symbolic communication in embodied agents.
