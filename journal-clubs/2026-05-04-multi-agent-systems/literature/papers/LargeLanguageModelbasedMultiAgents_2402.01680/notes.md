# Notes

## Why It Matters
This is a broad early map of LLM-based multi-agent systems. It is useful because it gives shared vocabulary for comparing systems that otherwise differ across roles, environments, communication structures, feedback loops, and application domains.

## When To Cite
Cite it for a general LLM-MA survey, for taxonomy language around interface/profiling/communication/capability acquisition, for examples of problem-solving versus world-simulation applications, and for early open problems in hallucination propagation, orchestration, scaling, multimodal environments, and benchmarks.
