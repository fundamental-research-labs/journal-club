# Notes

## Why It Matters
SOTOPIA is a central benchmark for treating social intelligence as interaction rather than question answering. It forces language agents to manage private goals, partial observability, partner behavior, relationship maintenance, norms, secrets, and material tradeoffs within one episode, which makes it useful evidence for both multi-agent evaluation and human-agent interaction work.

## When To Cite
Cite it when discussing interactive social intelligence benchmarks, role-playing language agents, social simulation environments, LLM-as-judge evaluation of conversations, human-vs-model social behavior, and evaluation dimensions beyond task success.

## Key Terms
SOTOPIA; SOTOPIA-EVAL; SOTOPIA-hard; social intelligence; goal completion; believability; knowledge gain; secret keeping; relationship impact; social rules; financial and material benefits; role-play agents; dyadic interaction; mixed-motive Markov game; GPT-4 evaluator.
