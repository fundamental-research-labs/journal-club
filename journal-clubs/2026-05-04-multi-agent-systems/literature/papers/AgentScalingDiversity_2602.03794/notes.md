# Notes

## Why It Matters
This paper gives a compact theory for why "more agents" often under-delivers: the key variable is non-redundant, task-relevant evidence. It is useful for designing multi-agent systems that choose heterogeneity deliberately instead of spending compute on repeated similar calls.

## When To Cite
Cite it for agent-count diminishing returns, diversity-aware MAS design, effective channels, heterogeneous voting/debate, and label-free diversity measurement with `K*`.

## Key Terms
Effective channels; usable evidence; complementarity rate; `K*`; entropy effective rank; homogeneous scaling; model diversity; persona diversity; correct-path diversity; vote; debate.
