# Notes

## Why it matters

DELEGATE-52 turns "can I trust the model to edit my files?" into a measurable long-horizon preservation problem. It is especially useful for arguing that single-turn editing quality is not enough for delegated work.

## When to cite/use

Cite for benchmarks of document-editing reliability, long-horizon artifact degradation, round-trip evaluation, domain-specific similarity metrics, and caution around agentic editing harnesses.

## Key terms

- DELEGATE-52
- delegated work
- round-trip relay
- reconstruction score
- sparse critical failures
- domain-specific evaluation
