# Notes

## Why It Matters
MLGym is a useful bridge between code-agent benchmarks and automated-science evaluations. It gives AI research agents a reproducible environment where progress is measured through executable ML experiments, task-specific artifacts, and comparable aggregate metrics instead of only issue-resolution tests or subjective idea quality.

## When To Cite
Cite it for Gym-style environments for LLM research agents, benchmarks for automated ML experimentation, Level-1 baseline-improvement evaluations, flexible artifact grading, performance-profile/AUP aggregation across heterogeneous metrics, and empirical comparisons of frontier models on AI research tasks.
