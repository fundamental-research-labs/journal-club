# Notes

## Why It Matters
RIAL/DIAL is an early anchor for deep emergent communication: it moves beyond tabular or hand-engineered protocols and shows how recurrent deep RL agents can learn task-specific messages in partially observable cooperative settings. Its central lesson is still useful: if training is centralized, communication can be treated as a differentiable interface for learning, then constrained back to a discrete channel for decentralized execution.

## When To Cite
Cite this paper for RIAL, DIAL, differentiable inter-agent communication, centralized training with decentralized execution, learned discrete protocols, communication under partial observability, or benchmark tasks such as the switch riddle and MNIST communication games. It is also a good historical reference when contrasting discrete emergent communication with continuous communication architectures such as CommNet.
