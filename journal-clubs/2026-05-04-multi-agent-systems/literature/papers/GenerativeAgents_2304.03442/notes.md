# Notes

## Why It Matters
This paper is one of the clearest early blueprints for persistent LLM agents. Its lasting contribution is not just the Smallville demo, but the architecture pattern: store experience as natural language, retrieve salient context, synthesize reflections, and maintain plans that can be revised as the world changes. It also gives useful evidence that believable multi-agent behavior requires more than prompting a model with a persona.

## When To Cite
Cite it for LLM agents with long-term memory, reflection, and planning; believable NPCs; interactive social simulation; emergent behavior in agent societies; natural-language user intervention in simulated worlds; and early empirical evaluation of agent believability through interview-style probes and ablations.
