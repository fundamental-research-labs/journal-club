## Coverage Notes

- Covers central seed frameworks plus missing adjacent candidates for automatic team construction, agent platforms, workflow search, and communication-topology optimization.
- Debate and aggregation coverage includes debate strategy analysis, round-table consensus, pairwise fusion, multi-agent verification, and domain-specific medical/math reasoning systems.
- Coordination coverage spans dynamic routing, decentralized/evolutionary coordination, actor-critic control, communication pruning, and out-of-sync recovery.
- Society and simulation coverage emphasizes generative-agent populations, social networks, recommender systems, finance, public administration, opinion dynamics, and research communities.
- Embodied and game settings are included where multiple agents coordinate, compete, or communicate in shared environments; purely single-agent web/computer-use benchmarks were intentionally omitted.
- Watchlist items are mostly bridge papers with graph/tree/parallel reasoning patterns or incomplete metadata that may still inform agent topology and orchestration design.
