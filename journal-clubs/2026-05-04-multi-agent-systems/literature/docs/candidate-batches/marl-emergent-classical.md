## Coverage Notes

- Covered the existing historical seed anchors explicitly, then prioritized missing candidates around CTDE, value factorization, credit assignment, opponent modeling, and sequence-model MARL.
- Emergent-communication coverage leans on the local `2006.02419` survey bibliography and emphasizes evaluation failures, drift, compositionality, and natural-language alignment rather than every referential-game variant.
- Social-dilemma and human-AI coordination entries focus on papers that still inform LLM-agent cooperation, partner modeling, conventions, and ad-hoc teamwork.
- Benchmark coverage includes environment APIs and suites that shaped MARL practice: SMAC/SMACv2, OpenSpiel, PettingZoo, Melting Pot, MAgent, Neural MMO, football, Pommerman, and SMARTS.
- Classical MAS coverage is selective: BDI, commitments, joint intentions, shared plans, KQML/ACL semantics, Gaia, Contract Net, and Dec-POMDP/I-POMDP foundations. I avoided duplicating the existing Wooldridge/Jennings 1995, Rao/Georgeff 1995, and Shoham/Leyton-Brown 2008 candidate entries.
- Some older classical entries use venue IDs instead of DOIs where stable DOI metadata was not immediately available.
