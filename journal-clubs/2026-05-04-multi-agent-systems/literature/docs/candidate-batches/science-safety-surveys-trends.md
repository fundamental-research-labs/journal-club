## Coverage Notes

- Covered 100 papers: 25 existing central seeds plus 75 additional candidates or watchlist items.
- Scientific discovery coverage includes AI co-scientists, autonomous labs, chemistry/materials agents, research ideation, data-driven discovery, reproducibility, and ML-research benchmarks.
- Medical/domain coverage emphasizes agent societies and collaborative diagnosis rather than generic medical LLM QA.
- Safety/security coverage focuses on tool-using agents, prompt injection, data exfiltration, harmful multi-step tasks, cyber capability evaluation, and agent-specific defense papers.
- Survey coverage includes general LLM-agent surveys, LLM-MAS surveys, communication taxonomies, scientific-agent surveys, and application-specific reviews.
- Uncertainty remains highest for very recent 2026 watchlist items and some late-2025 medical/security preprints; these should be reviewed before promotion.
